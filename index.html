Welcome to Pivot Pharmacy Limited

PIVOT Pharmacy Limited is a cutting-edge sourcing and distribution firm that specializes in providing exclusive representation and supply of top-quality pharmaceutical products. Our core pharmaceutical business is firmly rooted in Nigeria, where we have established a strong presence across the entire value chain in Pharmaceutical Products Distribution.

In addition to our core operations, we are also actively involved in research and development activities to better serve our customers. We offer Contract Research to pharmaceutical companies to assist with the introduction of new products to both retail and wholesale suppliers.

At PIVOT Pharmacy Limited, we are committed to delivering exceptional service and quality products to our customers. Our integrated approach to the pharmaceutical industry ensures that we are always at the forefront of innovation and customer satisfaction.
