The Pivot Pharmacy Story

A licensed Nigerian pharmaceutical company built on regulatory expertise, quality supply — and care that crosses borders.

Who we are

Care first, in everything we supply

Pivot Pharmacy Limited is a pharmaceutical company headquartered in Lagos, Nigeria. For over eight years we have represented and supplied top-quality pharmaceutical products across Nigeria, working with retail and wholesale partners nationwide.

Guided by our motto — “We care. God heals.” — we have grown beyond distribution into contract research, medical tourism and international study services, connecting Nigerians to world-class healthcare and education abroad.

  • Licensed by the Pharmacists Council of Nigeria (PCN)
  • Exclusive product representation & nationwide distribution
  • Partner hospitals and universities across Europe & Asia
  • A superintendent pharmacist at the helm of quality
