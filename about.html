About Us

Who We Are

PIVOT Pharmacy Limited is an innovative sourcing & distribution company involved in both exclusive representation and supply of a wide range of quality pharmaceutical products. Utilizing over 8 years of experience and success in the Pharmaceutical Sector, we have created a customized and unique project management team, designed to fulfill our customers' requirements with the highest quality products and regulatory requirements.

We are committed to meeting the needs of our customers through the provision of tailored solutions, including R&D services and Contract Research, to introduce new products to both retail and wholesale suppliers. Besides our core pharmaceutical business in Nigeria, we are an integrated supplier with a strong presence across the entire value chain in Pharmaceutical Products Distribution.

As a licensed company, we specialize in providing comprehensive sourcing options and expert regulatory support that fully complies with strict Pharmaceutical practice guidelines. Our operations are regulated by leading authorities such as the Pharmacist Council of Nigeria and the Association of Community Pharmacists of Nigeria, among others.

Our Mission

Our mission is to become the preferred supply partner to all of our customers nationwide and achieve sustained growth, through consistent delivery of innovative, customer-centric, world-class quality products.

Our Team

Our core team comprises highly qualified professionals with excellent academic accreditation and extensive experience in various aspects of the pharmaceutical industry. All our employees share a common vision of a better future for our customers and the continued success of our organization.

Our performance-driven work culture and accountable approach to work attract top professionals who are committed to strengthening our organization's image as one of the leaders in the healthcare industry.
