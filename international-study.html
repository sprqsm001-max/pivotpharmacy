International Study

Study in the Czech Republic

Pivot Pharmacy facilitates access to world-class educational opportunities in the Czech Republic. We connect prospective students with partner universities offering a wide range of programmes across all faculties and academic levels — from undergraduate through to doctorate.

Courses & Programmes

Courses from across University faculties and at all levels:

🎓

Doctorate Programs

PhD and professional doctorate opportunities at leading Czech universities in medicine, pharmacy, science and technology.

📚

Postgraduate Studies

Master's and specialist programmes across faculties including health sciences, engineering, business, and humanities.

🏛️

Undergraduate Degrees

Bachelor's degree programmes spanning a diverse range of disciplines at accredited Czech Republic institutions.

🌍

Exchange & Short Courses

Short-term study, exchange, and professional development programmes for working professionals.

🇨🇿 Why Czech Republic? — The Czech Republic is home to some of Central Europe's oldest and most respected universities. English-taught programmes, affordable living costs, and EU membership make it an excellent destination for international students.
